About

Lumi Island shows a small black island at the top of your screen with Lumi, a character you can talk to.

Using it: click the island for your PC at a glance, scroll on it for volume, right-click it for this menu, hover Lumi for the mic, and use the command bar shortcut to search apps or start a focus session.

Your data: settings live in config.json, your note in notes.txt and your focus history in focus_log.json, all next to the app. API keys are kept in Windows Credential Manager. Nothing is sent anywhere except when you talk to Lumi: typed chat goes to the AI provider you chose (Gemini, Claude, OpenAI, or your own server); in standard voice mode your voice goes to Google to become text and your words go to that provider; in live mode your voice streams to Gemini while the mic is on.
