## Design Specificity Verdict

LLM assessment: more distinctive than most utilities, but all of it comes from Lumi. The pill is a straight Dynamic Island and the panels are generic widget-kit pieces. Lumi is genuinely authored: a three-layer mood controller with minimum on-screen times, an interrupt rule for bad news and a watchdog; body language mapped to real steps of a request. The two barely meet: inside the panels Lumi is a static blob, and the pill never reflects its mood. The biggest missed opportunity: Lumi already shows the status, yet the pill also spells it out in text.

Deterministic scan: source scan found 4 issues. low-contrast: onboarding "Next" button (.btn-primary, ui/island.css:348), 12px white on #4C8DFF, 3.2:1 against 4.5:1 (true positive, missed by the design review). cramped-padding on the three dashboard stat tiles: false positives (contents centred in a fixed 76px height). Running app over CDP: 0 findings (small island). Test page at 1280 and 390 wide: 0 findings. Overlay on the test page: "No anti-patterns found" for the small island; scans of expanded panels inconclusive because the hidden browser pane left the panels invisible.

## Overall Impression
A disciplined, calm overlay with an unusually honest AI layer. But the small pill can't fit its own status text, it jitters while Lumi works, and the character sits on the user's work all day without earning that space. Biggest opportunity: make Lumi carry the status and disappear when it has nothing to say.

## What's Working
1. The mood controller (ui/lumi-state.js): no stuck "thinking", no flicker; tilt rather than a red body for errors.
2. Transparency and control over requests: tool steps become labels, ✓/✕ receipts in chat, Stop in two places, Esc as universal cancel; late replies from a stopped request can't come back.
3. Motion and colour discipline: one spring curve, closing faster than opening, colour only for meaning, Off/Subtle/Full following Windows, Lumi rests after 2 minutes idle.

## Priority Issues
1. [P1] Status labels don't fit the small pill. With Stop showing the label gets ~114px; "Checking your note…", "Adding to your note…", "Controlling music…" wrap and clip (35px tall in a 34px pill). #status-text has no single-line rule. Fix: single line with an ellipsis; size the pill to the measured label (at least ~236px with Stop); shorter labels ("Reading note"). Command: $impeccable layout
2. [P1] The pill jitters during a request: status, then toast, then status; width flips 197↔241px every ~300ms and Stop vanishes during toasts. Fix: while a request runs keep the status shape and show receipts inside it; one confirmation when it ends; no toasts for lookups. Command: $impeccable polish
3. [P1] Lumi covers the user's work all day: 60px below the pill, catches clicks there, covers titles, tabs and terminal output in real screenshots. Fix: tuck up to peek over the pill edge while idle, drop down only when it has something to say or the pointer approaches; or an auto-hide setting. Command: $impeccable shape
4. [P2] Errors are dead ends (missing key, provider not answering, timeout). Fix: inline buttons (Open Settings, Retry) in chat; clickable card instead of an unclickable bubble for watchdog and hotkey errors. Command: $impeccable harden
5. [P2] Keyboard/screen-reader gaps plus one contrast failure: small-pill activities hidden from screen readers; island spoken label wrong when Enter opens music/focus; command bar selection not announced (no aria-activedescendant); focus chips without arrow keys; aria-expanded hard-coded true; chat and command inputs remove their focus outline; "Next" button 3.2:1. Fix: spoken label from the current activity, announce selection, arrow keys between chips, focus rings, darker blue or larger bold text. Command: $impeccable audit

## Persona Red Flags
- Alex (power user): two clicks for a non-default focus length; panels close after a stray mouse movement; no in-panel shortcuts; three of five dashboard tiles repeat what Windows shows; Quit one misclick from "Hide 1 hour".
- Sam (keyboard/screen reader/low vision): live activities silent; command-bar selection not announced; chips not arrow-navigable; 11–12px grey secondary text throughout; bubbles disappear on a 3.5–6s timer; "✕" is a text character.
- Jordan (first-timer): every gesture invisible after the tour, tour can't be replayed; first chat without a key ends in an error with no button; clicking the pill opens a different panel depending on hidden state; Lumi's colours have no legend (amber can look like a warning).

## Minor Observations
- Menu shows three equal pills; Quit should be separated and de-emphasised.
- Speech bubble's 5-line clamp silently cuts long spoken replies.
- Small music view shows the title only, never the artist.
- User chat bubble (#1E3A66) is a fourth shade of blue.
- .mood-focus dims the whole Lumi container including the mic button.
- Stale comment "a typed question is with Gemini" (ui/island.js:35).
- Test page closed the chat soon after a reply arrived with the pointer away; check a reply is never closed before it's read.

## Questions to Consider
- If Lumi's mood already says listening/thinking/working, why does the pill spell it out? Could the text be only for screen readers?
- What would Lumi look like if it appeared only when it had something to say?
- Is RAM/battery/network the job of the expanded view, or a placeholder? What would a focus-and-music-first expanded state look like?
- Should Lumi live inside the panels instead of parked under them?
